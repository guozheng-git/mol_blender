# Beta service terms

Blender Render creates molecular illustrations from files you are authorized to process. You retain rights to your inputs and rendered outputs. Do not submit files you lack permission to share with the selected compute operator.

The shared beta provides limited, credit-funded compute with no payment method, automatic replenishment, service-level agreement, or guarantee of continued availability. Per-account limits are one active job, five renders and twenty GPU minutes per UTC day, a ten-minute job timeout, and a maximum dimension of 4096 pixels. The ordinary output default is 2048 pixels. Invalid, failed, and cancelled executions may consume their reserved allowance.

Do not evade quotas, access another user's jobs, or use uploads to execute arbitrary code. Only PSE, CIF, and PDB molecular inputs are supported. Rendering does not validate a structural model's scientific conclusions.
