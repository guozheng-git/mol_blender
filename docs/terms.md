# Beta service terms

Blender Render creates molecular illustrations from files you are authorized to process. You retain rights to your inputs and rendered outputs. Do not submit files you lack permission to share with the selected compute operator.

The shared beta provides limited, credit-funded compute with no payment method, automatic replenishment, service-level agreement, or guarantee of continued availability. Each account receives ten completed renders per UTC day. Pending jobs reserve daily slots; failures and cancellations release those slots. There is no upload-count or daily GPU-minute quota. The verified owner account is exempt from the daily render limit during testing. All accounts remain subject to project funds, queue capacity, a ten-minute job timeout, and a maximum dimension of 4096 pixels. Output defaults to 2048 pixels. Conversion attempts and failed or cancelled executions can still incur compute costs against the project budget.

Do not evade quotas, access another user's jobs, or use uploads to execute arbitrary code. Only PSE, CIF, and PDB molecular inputs are supported. Rendering does not validate a structural model's scientific conclusions.
